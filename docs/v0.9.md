# v0.9 — Conservative contrast and spectral scattering

This release preserves fine detail in readability analysis and carries wavelength-dependent refraction through every scattering level. It also exposes selection-lens lift profiles and improves the size estimate for grouped surfaces.

## Material changes

- Contrast probes copy the foreground region at native render resolution, then conservatively reduce RGB minima and maxima on the GPU. Every pixel of that rectangular region contributes; narrow lines and isolated dots no longer disappear between a fixed grid of point samples.
- The reported ratio is a conservative lower bound for the last captured region, including a possible intermediate luminance. It can understate contrast, particularly when unrelated channel extrema form synthetic colors. It does not cover future frames, custom ink or content outside that region.
- Readback remains asynchronous and bounded. A shared reduction workspace is reused across surfaces; only two normalized RGB bounds per surface reach the CPU solver. Analysis draws and scratch storage are included in renderer statistics.
- Red/blue ray coordinates now apply to sharp and both cached scattering levels, in artistic and Cauchy modes. Dispersion remains active at full frost. Default bevel remains zero; no extra colored border is painted.
- Size adaptation uses member-rectangle union area, excluding empty gaps and counting overlaps once. Arbitrary silhouette coverage remains an approximation.

## Developer APIs

`evaluateContrastBounds` accepts conservative RGB extrema from a custom compositor. `measureMaterialFootprint` returns bounding dimensions and occupied rectangle area. `resolveMaterialForSize` accepts an optional area for application-supplied silhouette measurements. Existing calls remain compatible.

Selection lift scale/offset, lifted material, tracking/release springs and interaction physics are configurable through `bindLiquidSelection`. Existing defaults remain unchanged and are not claimed as measured native constants.

## Verification and scope

New real-pixel regressions cover 1–2 pixel stripes, horizontal lines, isolated dots, odd-sized regions, insufficient protection and dispersion at full scattering. See [Validation](validation.md) and [Performance](performance.md) for the complete results and measured environment.

Read the [dispersion guide](dispersion.md), [readability contract](readability.md), [size response](material-response.md) and [selection motion](motion.md). The compositor remains RGBA8/sRGB with cached scattering and screen-space optics. Native matched-scene image/motion error, HDR/EDR and Android/120 Hz performance remain unmeasured.
